What Does Blood Type Mean?

Despite the importance of distinguishing between blood types, they weren’t discovered until 1901 by Karl Landsteiner, an Austrian scientist. Since blood type matching is necessary for blood transfusions, prior to the discovery of blood types, doctors didn’t know why transfusions saved some lives while causing others to die. The different blood types we’re now aware of are decided based on the antigens present in the blood. Antigens are substances that can trigger an immune response in your body if they detect something foreign or harmful. The antigens present in different blood types are the reason not all blood types are interchangeable, and you can only receive blood that’s a match for your specific type.
